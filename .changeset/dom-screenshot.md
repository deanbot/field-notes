Screenshots: each note gets a screenshot of what's on screen, taken as the widget opens (without the widget in it) and again after a pick. Passwords and `[data-field-notes-mask]` content are painted over. The form shows a thumbnail that can be removed. `screenshot: 'off'` (or `data-screenshot="off"` on the script tag) turns them off.
